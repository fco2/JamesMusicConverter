## 📱 How to Use

### **Step 1: Choose Your Mode**

Select what you want to download:

- **Video (MP4)** - Download the video file
- **Audio (MP3)** - Convert to audio only

💡 **Tip:** Video mode is selected by default for convenience.

### **Step 2: Enter or Paste URL**

1. Copy the video URL from your browser
2. Tap the paste icon (📋) in the text field
3. The app will automatically fetch video information

**Supported URL formats:**

```
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...
https://youtu.be/...
https://www.tiktok.com/@user/video/...
https://www.instagram.com/p/...
https://twitter.com/user/status/...
```

### **Step 3: Preview Your Video**

Once the URL is entered, you'll see:

- A loading indicator while fetching info
- Then a preview card with thumbnail and details
- Platform badge showing the source
- "Ready to download" status with checkmark

### **Step 4: Download/Convert**

Tap the big download button:

- For videos: "Download [Platform] Video"
- For audio: "Convert [Platform] to MP3"

### **Step 5: Track Progress**

Watch the animated progress screen:

- Circular progress indicator
- Percentage complete
- Current status message
- Linear progress bar
- Option to cancel if needed

### **Step 6: Access Your File**

On completion, you'll see:

- File details (name, size, duration)
- Thumbnail preview
- Quick actions:
    - ▶️ Play - Open in your media player
    - 📤 Share - Send to friends
    - 📁 Open Location - View in file manager
    - 🏠 Download Another - Return to home

## ❗ Troubleshooting

### **"Could not fetch video information"**

- **Check:** Is the URL correct and publicly accessible?
- **Try:** Opening the URL in your browser first
- **Solution:** Use Advanced Options if login is required

### **"Network connection error"**

- **Check:** Your internet connection
- **Try:** Switching from WiFi to mobile data (or vice versa)
- **Solution:** Disable VPN if active

### **"Video is private or restricted"**

- **Reason:** The video requires authentication
- **Solution:** Use Advanced Options with:
    - Username/password, OR
    - Browser cookies

### **"Permission denied" or storage errors**

- **Check:** App permissions in Settings
- **Try:** Granting storage permissions
- **Solution:** Ensure device has free space (at least 500MB)

### **Preview not loading**

- **Why:** Network slow or yt-dlp initializing
- **Wait:** A few seconds for first load
- **Note:** You can still download without preview

### **Wrong video in preview**

- **Check:** Did you paste the correct URL?
- **Fix:** Clear the field and paste again
- **Note:** Some platforms redirect URLs

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

### **For Best Results:**

1. ✅ Use the **full video URL** (not shortened links)
2. ✅ Ensure **stable internet** connection
3. ✅ Check the **preview** before downloading
4. ✅ Use **browser cookies** for private content
5. ✅ Keep the **app open** during download

### **Performance Tips:**

- First download may be slower (yt-dlp initialization)
- Subsequent downloads are much faster
- Video mode downloads are larger (longer download)
- Audio mode is faster and uses less storage

### **Quality Tips:**

- **Videos:** Downloaded at best available quality (usually 1080p)
- **Audio:** Converted at 320kbps (highest MP3 quality)
- **Thumbnails:** High resolution when available

## 🆘 Getting Help

### **Still Having Issues?**

1. **Check the error message** - It now provides specific suggestions
2. **Try a different video** - Test if the problem is URL-specific
3. **Restart the app** - Sometimes fixes initialization issues
4. **Check permissions** - Ensure storage access is granted
5. **Free up space** - Make sure device has storage available

### **Common Questions:**

**Q: Why does preview take a few seconds?**  
A: The app is fetching metadata from the video platform. First load may take longer.

**Q: Can I download playlists?**  
A: Currently, you need to download videos individually.

**Q: What's the maximum video length?**  
A: No app-imposed limit. Longer videos take more time and storage.

**Q: Why is audio quality 320kbps?**  
A: This is the highest quality for MP3 format, perfect for music.

**Q: Can I choose video quality?**  
A: The app downloads best available quality by default.
