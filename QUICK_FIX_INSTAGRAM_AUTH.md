# Quick Fix: Instagram "Authentication Required" Error

## The Problem

You see this error:

```
Instagram Authentication Required
This content requires login...
```

## The Solution (2 Minutes)

### Option 1: Browser Cookies (Recommended) 🌟

1. **Login to Instagram in your browser**
    - Open Chrome (or Firefox/Edge)
    - Go to instagram.com
    - Log in normally

2. **Configure the app**
    - Open James Music Converter
    - Paste your Instagram URL
    - Tap **"Advanced Options"**
    - Enable ☑️ **"Extract cookies from browser"**
    - Type browser name: `chrome` (or `firefox`, `edge`)

3. **Download**
    - Tap Download button
    - Success! ✨

### Option 2: Username & Password

1. Open app → Paste URL
2. Tap **"Advanced Options"**
3. Enter your Instagram **username**
4. Enter your Instagram **password**
5. Download

> ⚠️ **Note:** May not work with 2FA. Use Option 1 if you have 2FA enabled.

## Supported Browsers

Type exactly as shown:

- `chrome` - Google Chrome
- `firefox` - Mozilla Firefox
- `edge` - Microsoft Edge
- `safari` - Safari (Mac)

## Why This Happens

Instagram restricts access to:

- Private accounts
- Follower-only content
- Stories from accounts you follow

The app needs your login to access this content on your behalf.

## Is It Safe? 🔒

**Yes!** The app:

- ✅ Uses cookies stored locally on your device
- ✅ Never sends your cookies anywhere
- ✅ Only accesses Instagram on your behalf
- ✅ Same as using your browser

## Still Not Working?

1. **Make sure you're logged in** - Open Instagram in your browser and verify you're logged in
2. **Check browser name** - Must be exactly `chrome`, `firefox`, etc. (lowercase)
3. **Try different browser** - If Chrome doesn't work, try Firefox
4. **Check content exists** - Make sure the Instagram post/story is still available
